# 2026 Advent Calendar

An interactive React + TypeScript + SVG Advent Calendar featuring an **always-evolving cabin & snowy yard wallpaper**. Every day from Day 1 through Day 24 unlocks a dedicated interactive SVG mini-activity that permanently transforms the main wallpaper scene.

## Running Locally

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the local Vite URL (default `http://localhost:5173`). Days unlock automatically at **00:01 SAST (`Africa/Johannesburg`)** starting **December 1, 2026**. Use **View Wallpaper** in the top bar to hide the 24-day grid and admire your evolving scene.

## All 24 Days
1. **Chop Down Tree** — Pick a species and chop down your tree in the snowy woods.
2. **Build Tree** — Assemble the tree inside the cabin with your chosen stand and height.
3. **Ornaments & Decorations** — Hang baubles, candy canes, bells, and garlands directly on the tree.
4. **Draw Christmas Photo** — Sketch holiday artwork on an interactive SVG canvas.
5. **Colour in Photo** — Fill and paint your Day 4 sketch with festive colors.
6. **Frame & Hang Photo** — Pick a rustic wood or ornate gold frame and hang it above the fireplace.
7. **Light the Fireplace** — Stack logs and strike a match for a permanent flickering fire & warm room glow.
8. **Start Snow Machine** — Press the button to start permanent animated snowfall across the calendar.
9. **Prep Gingerbread** — Mix, roll, cut, and bake spiced gingerbread house panels.
10. **Build Gingerbread House** — Assemble the walls, roof, snow-drift icing, and candies.
11. **Build Snowman** — Drag snowballs and stick arms into place in the snowy yard.
12. **Dress the Snowman** — Add a scarf, carrot nose, coal buttons, and top hat.
13. **String Outdoor Lights** — Click and drag across roofline hooks for twinkling outdoor lights.
14. **Winter Bird Feeder** — Build a wooden feeder and scatter seeds for a visiting winter bird.
15. **Display Gingerbread House** — Move your house to the windowsill or table with glowing candy windows.
16. **Decorate Cookies** — Pipe frosting and shake sprinkles onto star and tree cookies.
17. **Wrap Presents** — Cut paper, fold corners, and tie ribbons for gifts under the tree.
18. **Holiday Wish Letter** — Write a message, seal it with wax, and tuck it onto the mantle (clickable anytime).
19. **Hang Stockings** — Customize initials, colors, and knit patterns along the fireplace mantle.
20. **Repair Music Box** — Assemble 3 interlocking brass gears to unlock Web Audio holiday chimes on the home screen.
21. **Light Outdoor Lanterns** — Light the pathway lanterns to guide Santa's sleigh.
22. **Prep Eggnog** — Whisk, spice, and garnish a steaming holiday mug on the windowsill.
23. **Milk by the Hearth** — Pour a glass of milk next to the Day 16 cookies by the hearth.
24. **Place the Tree Star** — Guide the glowing star topper to crown the Christmas tree!
